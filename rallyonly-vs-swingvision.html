RallyOnly vs SwingVision: Rally Highlights or Match Analysis?

Compare iPhone tennis editing, analysis features and dated US pricing. Choose rally trimming or a broader scoring and statistics workflow.

Written by RallyOnly’s developer. This is a product comparison, not an independent review. Public pricing checked 15 November 2026.

RallyOnly is an option if you already record tennis matches and want to remove downtime and share highlights on your iPhone. SwingVision is the broader option when you want shot statistics, scoring and line-calling tools as well as highlights. They are not feature-equivalent.

What the prices mean

Sources: RallyOnly’s US App Store listing and SwingVision’s US App Store listing, checked on 15 November 2026. Prices vary by country, eligibility and offer, and are not a quote for every customer.

SwingVision’s description also presents its annual price as approximately US$14.99 per month. An annual equivalent is not proof that a separately billed monthly plan costs that amount. Its listing contains several in-app purchase offers; confirm the actual plan and billing period in the purchase sheet. We have not verified a current monthly-billed SwingVision checkout price.

RallyOnly Pro removes the monthly processing limit and export watermark. The free tier is not an unlimited free plan. Trial eligibility, length and the price after a trial are shown before purchase.

Choose for your actual workflow

  • Choose RallyOnly for a focused edit: import, inspect detected rallies, correct boundaries and export a reel. See the trimming tutorial.
  • Consider SwingVision for broader analysis: check its scoring, statistics and line-calling requirements against your devices and recording setup.

Limits of this comparison

We did not run a controlled head-to-head accuracy or speed benchmark. Rally detection can miss play or include waiting; check and correct the result. No claim is made that RallyOnly detects every point or is more accurate than SwingVision.

Local analysis does not mean every action is network-free. A recording stored only in iCloud needs to download first, Apple handles subscriptions, and sharing through another service follows that service’s terms. Read RallyOnly’s privacy policy and storage and troubleshooting guidance.
