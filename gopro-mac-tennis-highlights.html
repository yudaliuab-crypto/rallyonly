1. Why action-camera match editing stalls on Mac

Recording a match with a GoPro, DJI Osmo Action or Insta360 solves the phone-overheating problem, but creates a desk problem when you get home: two hours of 1080p or 4K footage sitting across multiple 4 GB files on an SD card.

Most players end up with one of three bad options:

  • Manual timeline scrubbing in iMovie, Final Cut or Premiere — which takes roughly 1:1 with match length.
  • Cloud upload tools — uploading 15–30 GB over home Wi-Fi just to wait on a server queue, often with high monthly fees.
  • Importing into Photos — which duplicates massive files inside your system Photo Library before you even decide which points are worth keeping.

Starting in version 1.40, RallyOnly runs natively on Apple silicon Macs and reads local video files straight from Finder or an external drive — keeping the editing local and skipping the extra copy.

2. Camera prep: GoPro, DJI & Insta360

Any standard flat .mp4 or .mov video works. A few settings make the cut much cleaner before you even hit record:

  • Placement & lens: Mount behind the baseline (or above/against the back glass for padel) with both players and both baselines visible. Use Linear or Wide rather than extreme SuperView fisheye, which shrinks the far player to a few pixels.
  • Resolution & frame rate: 1080p at 30 fps or 60 fps is ideal. You do not need 4K for rally detection, and 1080p keeps SD card sizes and analysis times much smaller.
  • GoPro & DJI Osmo Action / Pocket: Output standard .mp4 files directly — no conversion needed.
  • Insta360: RallyOnly does not read raw 360° .insv files. If you shoot in 360° mode, reframe and export a flat court-facing .mp4 in Insta360 Studio first, then drop that MP4 into RallyOnly. (If you shoot in single-lens MP4 mode, you can use it directly.)

3. Step 1: Import without copying huge files

  1. Decide where the originals will live: If you only want to pull out today's highlight reel and discard the raw footage afterwards, you can drop files straight from your SD card reader or external SSD. If you want to reopen the condensed match later inside RallyOnly, copy your camera folder once into a permanent Mac folder (for example ~/Movies/Matches/) first.
  2. Open RallyOnly on your Mac (or iPad/iPhone using Files).
  3. Drag and drop the .mp4 / .mov files straight from Finder onto the RallyOnly start window — or click Choose a video › Files and pick them in the file dialog.
Zero duplicate storage: RallyOnly links to each file where it already sits using a macOS security-scoped bookmark. It does not copy a 12 GB match into its own container. Just remember not to unplug an SD card or rename the file if you still want to play unsaved cuts from that session later.

4. Step 2: Handling split 4 GB GoPro chapters

GoPro and many action cameras split long matches into chapters (for example GX010012.MP4, GX020012.MP4, GX030012.MP4).

  • Queue the whole match in one drag: Select all chapter files together in Finder and drop them onto RallyOnly. It analyses them one after another automatically, creating one session per file, while you watch the chapters that have already finished.
  • Build one combined highlight reel across chapters: While each chapter currently stays its own session, you can bookmark the best rallies from Chapter 1, Chapter 2 and Chapter 3 into Saved rallies, then combine them under Collections into a single full-match highlight video.

5. Step 3: Fast keyboard review, scoreboard & export

Once analysis finishes, you get the condensed match, an automatic highlight reel, every rally ranked, and your serve list. On Mac (or an iPad with a keyboard), you can review and score a whole set without touching the mouse:

  • Playback: Space to play/pause, ← / → to jump 3 seconds, , / . to step one frame at a time (5 free turns per session, unlimited with Pro).
  • Navigate & pick: ↑ / ↓ for previous/next rally, A to tick or untick a rally for your custom reel, F for full screen.
  • Match scoreboard: Switch to Score mode and press 1 or 2 to mark who won each point (or start mid-match with Set score). Pro exports render the scoreboard directly onto the video.
  • Export straight to disk: Click Share and choose Save to Folder… to write your finished 16:9 YouTube cut, 9:16 Shorts/Reels/TikTok cut, or 1:1 clip directly into a Finder folder — or export one file per rally.

6. Honest limits & requirements

  • Hardware: Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later, macOS 14+), iPad (iPadOS 17+), or iPhone (iOS 17+). Intel Macs, Windows and Android are not supported.
  • Multi-file sessions: Dropping three GoPro chapters creates three sessions (you merge your favourite clips via Collections).
  • No cloud, no line calls: RallyOnly does not record, track ball speed or call lines — it only finds rallies, removes dead time and lets you add a manual scoreboard.
  • Pricing: Free for 4 new videos/sessions a month with every feature (free exports carry a small RallyOnly watermark). RallyOnly Pro removes the monthly limit and watermark, and one subscription covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac under the same Apple Account.
