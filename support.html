Getting the best results

RallyOnly finds the play by listening for ball strikes and watching for movement on the court, so where you put the phone decides how good the edit is.

  • Stand the phone behind the baseline, a few metres back, so both players and the whole court are in frame.
  • As high as you can — a fence post or a bag on a bench beats the ground.
  • Do not film from the side of the net. Players walking past the lens look exactly like play to any camera.
  • Landscape or portrait both work. Landscape is what most of the testing was done on, and it fits the shape of a court.
  • Nothing to set for doubles — the app works out how many players are on court from the video itself.

Common questions

How long does an edit take? Roughly a ninth of the recording's length on a recent iPhone — about four minutes for a half-hour session. Keep the app open while it works.

Does my video get uploaded? No. Nothing leaves your phone — see the privacy policy.

Why did it miss a rally? Usually the camera angle. If the recording was made from the side of the court, or very close to a player, there is much less for the app to work with. Send us the details and we will look at it.

Why does an old session say the original is gone? The condensed cut and the highlight reel are not saved as separate video files — they play straight out of your original recording, which is why they appear in seconds and take almost no space. Delete that recording from Photos and they have nothing to play. Two things survive it: anything you bookmarked or shared, and any export made with Keep in this session switched on, which saves it as the session's own file.

It says no original is linked. That session was imported before the app had permission to read your photo library, so there is nothing recorded to find the video again. Allow access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, then import the recording once more.
