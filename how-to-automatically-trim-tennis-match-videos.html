How to Automatically Trim Tennis Match Videos

Use RallyOnly on iPhone to detect rallies, remove downtime, correct cuts and export a tennis highlight reel without uploading the recording.

Written by RallyOnly’s developer. Instructions checked against the existing app workflow; updated 15 November 2026.

Record a match with your iPhone’s Camera app, choose it in RallyOnly, then review the detected rallies and export a highlight reel. The video analysis runs locally on the iPhone. You can adjust a cut rather than manually editing the whole recording.

On this page
  1. Prepare your recording
  2. Choose the video
  3. Review and correct the cut
  4. Pick your highlights
  5. Export and keep what you need

1. Prepare your recording

You need an iPhone running iOS 17 or later. RallyOnly currently has no Android, iPad-specific or web app. Film with the normal Camera app; RallyOnly does not record the match itself.

Place the phone behind the baseline with the full court and players visible, preferably elevated. A poor angle can cause missed rallies or false cuts. Follow the filming guide.

Make sure the recording is available in Photos. Footage from another camera can be used once it is in your Photos library. If the original is only in iCloud, let the iPhone download it first.

2. Choose the video in RallyOnly

Open RallyOnly, tap Choose a video, and select your recording from Photos. Allow access to the video you want to process. You do not need a RallyOnly account or a video upload.

Leave the app open while it finds rallies. Processing time varies with the iPhone and video; switching apps can pause analysis until you return. Multiple selected recordings are processed in sequence, each as a separate session.

3. Review and correct the cut

On the results screen, watch the condensed match or the automatically selected highlight reel. Open a rally to check where it begins and ends. If a cut starts too early or stops too late, use its start/end controls to adjust it.

Check that the serve, contact and end of the point are included. Automatic detection is a starting point, not a guarantee of complete recall. Recheck a difficult camera angle before deleting any source footage.

4. Pick the rallies for your reel

Open Pick your highlights to browse the ranked rally list. Select the rallies you want and generate your collection. You can also keep individual bookmarked rallies for later. The automatic reel and a collection you select are different ways to review the same session.

5. Export and keep what you need

Use the save/share controls and choose 16:9 for landscape video or 9:16 for portrait sharing. Save the finished file to Photos or send it using the iPhone share sheet. Review the exported file, including the start and end of each point, before posting.

Keep the original until your clips are safely saved.

The condensed match and automatic reel can play from the original instead of being separate files. Deleting that recording from Photos can make unsaved cuts unavailable. Bookmarked rallies, generated collections and exports explicitly kept in the session have their own saved files. A file exported to Photos or another app is a separate copy there. See storage guidance.

What is free?

RallyOnly includes 4 new matches per month with watermarked exports. Pro removes the monthly processing limit and watermark. Current US pricing is US$4.99/month or US$34.99/year in the US App Store listing, checked 15 November 2026; local pricing and trial terms appear before purchase.

Does it work completely offline?

Rally analysis takes place on your iPhone rather than a RallyOnly server. An iCloud-only recording needs a download first, Apple may need a connection for purchases or restores, and online sharing requires the relevant service. Local processing is not a promise that every step is network-free. See the privacy policy.
