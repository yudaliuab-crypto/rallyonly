Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

RallyOnly does not collect, transmit or store any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Your videos

When you choose a video, RallyOnly reads it from your photo library and analyses it entirely on the device. The clips it produces are written to the app's own storage on your iPhone. No video, audio, image or frame ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of any kind, so there is no server to send anything to.

Photo library access

RallyOnly asks for permission to read your photo library so you can pick a video to edit, and to save a finished clip back if you choose to. It reads only the video you select.

Data stored on your device

Finished sessions, clips and their settings are kept in the app's storage so you can reopen them. Deleting a session in the app deletes those files; deleting the app removes all of them.

Children

RallyOnly is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
