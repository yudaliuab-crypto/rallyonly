How it works

  1. Film as you already do. Stand the phone behind the baseline with the whole court in frame and record with the Camera app.
  2. Pick the video in RallyOnly. A video model on the iPhone watches the court and marks where each point starts and ends. Pick several recordings at once if you like; they are analysed one after another.
  3. Watch, trim, share. Play the condensed match or the reel straight away, nudge any rally that starts too early or ends too late, and export in 16:9 or 9:16.

What you get

  • The condensed match: every point, none of the waiting.
  • A two-minute highlight reel: the best rallies, picked and cut for you.
  • Every rally ranked: tick the ones you want and build your own reel.
  • Your serves in one list.
  • Singles or doubles worked out from the video; nothing to set.

Why players pick it

  • Nothing leaves your phone. No upload, no account, no waiting for a server.
  • It doesn't fill your storage. RallyOnly reads the original in Photos instead of copying it, and the condensed match and reel play straight out of it.
  • You never lose a match to the app. It doesn't record, so a crash can't cost you a set. You film with the Camera app.
  • What you make stays yours. If you stop paying, nothing you exported or saved is taken back.

Getting a good cut

  • Put the phone behind the baseline, a few metres back, as high as you can: a fence post or a bag on a bench beats the ground.
  • Don't film from the side of the net. Players walking past the lens look like play to any camera.
  • Landscape or portrait both work; landscape fits the court best.

Questions

Does my video get uploaded? No. RallyOnly analyses the video on your iPhone and reads the original straight from Photos. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. See the privacy policy.

How long does it take? Roughly a ninth of the recording's length on a recent iPhone: about four minutes for a half-hour session.

What if it cuts a point wrong? Open the rally and move its start or end, a second or three at a time. Sending us the details helps too: rallyonly.feedback@gmail.com.

Can I use GoPro or other camera footage? Yes, once the video is in your Photos library. Each file becomes its own session for now.

Is it free? Four new matches a month are free with every feature; free exports carry a small watermark. RallyOnly Pro removes the limit and the watermark, and new subscribers can start with a free trial.

Android? No, iPhone only (iOS 17 or later).
