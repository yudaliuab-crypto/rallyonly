Filming tips

Camera angle decides the cut

RallyOnly watches the court on your device, so giving it a clear view of both ends makes every rally cleaner.

  • Stand the camera behind the baselineA few metres back so both players and the whole court stay in frame.
  • Mount it as high as you canA fence mount, tall tripod or bag on a bench beats setting it on the ground.
  • Do not film from the side of the netPlayers walking past the lens look just like active play to any camera.
  • Landscape fits best · Doubles is automaticLandscape or portrait both work, and there is nothing to toggle for singles vs doubles.

Read the full filming guide (mounts, battery & heat) →

Tennis Singles & Doubles

Clip to the back fence behind the baseline or place a tripod centred behind the court. Keep both baselines visible if space allows.

Padel Glass courts

Choose an angle above the glass or flush against it with minimal reflections so both halves of the court remain crisp and unobstructed.

Pickleball 4-player framing

For doubles, step back slightly wider so all four players and both kitchen lines stay inside the frame throughout fast dink exchanges.

Storage & Originals

Why edits appear in seconds — and what survives cleanup

RallyOnly avoids copying multi-gigabyte source videos unless you ask it to. Here is what depends on your original recording and what lives safely on its own.

Plays from original

Condensed match & Highlight reel

These are not stored as duplicate video files — they play straight out of your original recording in Photos or Files, which is why they open immediately and take almost no space.

  • Keep the original in place until you have exported or saved the clips you want.
  • If the original is deleted or moved, unsaved cuts will show that the original is no longer available.
Independent copies

What stays even without the original

These are saved as self-contained clips and keep playing even if you delete the original recording or run Delete videos only:

  • Rallies you bookmarked in Saved rallies
  • Collections you generated
  • Any export made with Keep in this session turned on
  • Videos you shared, saved back to Photos, or exported with Save to Folder… on Mac

Editing & Troubleshooting

How long does analysis take? Can I queue several videos?

Timing depends on your device, recording length and resolution. Keep RallyOnly open while it runs. You can pick several recordings at once — they are analysed one after another into separate sessions while you keep using the app. If you switch away, iOS or iPadOS may pause analysis and resume automatically when you come back.

Why did it miss a rally or cut a point slightly early/late?

Camera angle is almost always the cause (side-of-net views, strong glass glare in padel, or standing very close to one player). Open any rally to nudge its start or end a second or three at a time — or step frame by frame. If a clean behind-the-baseline angle still missed points, email us the details and we will look into it.

It says "no original is linked" or "original is gone"

No original is linked: that session was imported before RallyOnly had permission to read your photo library. Enable Photos access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, then re-import the recording.
Original is gone: the video was deleted from Photos or moved/renamed in Files. Bookmarked rallies, collections and saved exports are unaffected.

Export failed — what should I check?

Check that your device has enough free storage for the exported file and try again. If it still fails, tap Settings › Send feedback inside the app — the draft automatically includes the error code and free space at that moment (no video or filenames) so we can pinpoint the cause quickly.

Does my video ever get uploaded?

Never. Everything runs on your iPhone, iPad or Mac with no account and no RallyOnly server. Read the privacy policy for full details.

RallyOnly Pro & Billing

What is included in the free tier?

Four new matches every calendar month with every feature — condensed match, automatic highlight reel, ranked rally picker, scoreboard and exports (free exports carry a small watermark). Your monthly free count stays synced across your own Apple devices via Apple Keychain and resets automatically on the 1st of each month.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — new subscribers can start RallyOnly Pro with a free trial. Its exact length and the renewal price in your local currency are shown before you confirm, and Apple shows them again in the App Store purchase sheet.

How do I cancel or switch plans?

Open Settings › Manage subscription inside RallyOnly, or go directly to Apple's subscription settings: on iPhone/iPad, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Mac, App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or billing period ends to avoid renewal. Everything you made stays in the app.

I have an offer code — where do I enter it?

In RallyOnly, open Settings › Redeem offer code.

I switched devices or installed on iPad / Mac and Pro is missing

Make sure you are signed into the App Store with the same Apple Account you subscribed with, then tap Settings › Restore purchases.

Still stuck, or an edit came out wrong?

Write to us directly — real replies, no bot loop. For export issues, using Settings › Send feedback inside RallyOnly includes the error code so we can help faster.
