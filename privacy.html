Privacy Policy

Your videos stay on your device

RallyOnly has no account and no server. Your tennis, padel and pickleball recordings are read, analysed and saved on your iPhone, iPad or Mac — never uploaded — and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to write to us.

Last updated 21 November 2026 App Store label: Data Not Collected iPhone · iPad · Mac

No account, no server

No sign-in, no cloud processing and no RallyOnly backend for your footage to go to.

100% on-device

Detection, scoreboards and video exports run locally on your iPhone, iPad or Apple silicon Mac.

Zero tracking or SDKs

No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting libraries and no third-party code.

Apple-handled billing

Subscriptions and free trials run solely through Apple's App Store — we never see your payment details.

01 Your videos

When you pick or drop a video — from Photos, the Files app, or a local folder on your Mac — RallyOnly reads it and analyses it entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Any clips you choose to save inside a session are written to the app's own local storage on that device. RallyOnly never uploads your video, audio, images or frames anywhere. There is no RallyOnly server for them to go to.

What you do with a finished clip is yours to decide. If you use the share sheet to send it to someone, post it, save it back to Photos, or save it to a folder on Mac (Save to Folder…), it goes only to the destination or service you picked, under that service's own terms. If you use iCloud Photos or iCloud Drive, your recordings and anything you save there are synced by Apple, just as they were before you installed RallyOnly. Those are your Apple services, not ours, and we see nothing of them.

02 What we collect

Nothing is sent to us automatically. RallyOnly contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it has no server of its own to talk to. The only network traffic the app triggers is Apple's:

  • App Store — when you start a trial, subscribe, restore purchases or redeem an offer code.
  • iCloud Photos / iCloud Drive — when a recording you pick is stored in iCloud and has to be downloaded to your device first.

Two things are worth stating plainly rather than leaving under "nothing":

  • Apple crash reports. If you have Apple's Share Analytics switched on in your device's privacy settings, Apple may send crash reports for apps you use — including this one — to the developer in Apple's own aggregated form. You control that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements (or System Settings on Mac), and it happens independently of the app. RallyOnly contains no crash-reporting code of its own.
  • The feedback email. If you choose Settings › Send feedback, the app opens a pre-filled draft in Mail containing the app version, OS version, device model, four plain counters (how many sessions you have analysed, how many you shared, how many share sheets you closed without sending, and how many of this month's free sessions you have used) and — only if an export recently failed — the error code and free disk space at that moment. Nothing else: no file names, no dates, no video. It is an ordinary email you can read, edit or delete before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press send.

03 Subscriptions

RallyOnly Pro is sold, renewed, refunded and cancelled by Apple through the App Store, including any free trial or offer code, and works across your iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Mac under the same Apple Account. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account email or your name. The app checks whether Pro is active using the signed receipt Apple keeps on your device; that check does not involve us.

04 Photos & Files access

  • Photo library. RallyOnly asks for permission to read your photo library so you can pick recordings to edit (and so existing sessions can keep playing straight from the original without copying gigabytes), and to save a finished clip back to Photos when you choose to. It reads only the videos you select.
  • Files, external drives & drag-and-drop. When you pick a video from Files on iPhone or iPad, or pick/drag a local video file on Mac, Apple grants RallyOnly read access to that specific file only. RallyOnly keeps an Apple security-scoped bookmark locally on your device so you can reopen the session later without duplicating the source video. On Mac, Save to Folder… writes only to the folder you select.

05 Data stored on your device

ItemWhere it livesHow to remove it
Sessions, rally timelines, scoreboards & exported clips Local app storage on your device On device Delete a session, use Delete videos only (frees video space while keeping timelines, saved rallies & collections), use Delete everything, or delete the app.
Saved rallies & Collections Local app storage on your device On device Deleting a single session keeps its bookmarked rallies in Saved rallies. Remove them there, use Delete everything, or delete the app.
Preferences & local counters Local app storage on your device On device Removed when you delete the app.

None of your sessions or videos are backed up by us. If you delete the app or lose your device, sessions stored only inside the app go with it — export or save any clip you want to keep permanently.

06 Children

RallyOnly is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone automatically.
