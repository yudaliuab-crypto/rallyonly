How it works

Three steps, no editing

Most match recordings are more waiting than playing. RallyOnly keeps the play.

1

Film as you already do

Stand the phone behind the baseline with the whole court in frame and record with the Camera app.

2

Pick the video

A model on your device detects the start and end of rallies. Pick several recordings at once if you like.

3

Watch, trim, share

Play the condensed match or reel right away, inspect strokes frame by frame, add the match scoreboard, and export in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.

Filming tips

Get a clean cut

  • Behind the baselineA few metres back, with both players and the whole court in frame.
  • As high as you canA fence post or a bag on a bench beats the ground.
  • Not from the side of the netPlayers walking past the lens look like play to any camera.
  • Landscape or portraitBoth work; landscape fits the court best.

Questions

Before you try it

Does my video get uploaded?

No. RallyOnly analyses the video on your device, without uploading it or requiring an account. See the privacy policy.

How long does it take?

It depends on your device, recording length and format. Keep the app open during analysis.

How do I add a scoreboard to my match video?

Switch any rally to Score mode and tap who won each point, or start from any rally with Set score. Pro exports overlay the scoreboard on the video.

What if it cuts a point wrong?

Open the rally and move its start or end, a second or three at a time, or turn the frame wheel on the right of the video to inspect a stroke frame by frame. Sending us the details helps too: rallyonly.feedback@gmail.com.

Can I use GoPro or other camera footage?

Yes. You can use Photos. Version 1.40 adds Files import and file selection on Mac. Read the GoPro, DJI & Mac guide →

Is it free?

Four new matches a month are free with every feature; free exports carry a small watermark. RallyOnly Pro removes the limit and the watermark, and new subscribers can start with a free trial.

Android?

No. RallyOnly is distributed through Apple’s App Store.
