Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

RallyOnly has no account, no server and no network connection. Your videos are read, analysed and saved on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to write to us.

Your videos

When you choose a video, RallyOnly reads it from your photo library and analyses it entirely on the device. The clips it produces are written to the app's own storage on your iPhone. RallyOnly never uploads your video, audio, images or frames anywhere. There is no RallyOnly server for them to go to.

What you do with a finished clip is yours to decide. If you use the share sheet to send it to someone, post it, or save it back to Photos, it goes to whichever app or service you picked, under that service's own terms. If your photo library uses iCloud Photos, your recordings and anything you save back are synced by Apple, as they were before you installed this app. Those are your services, not ours, and we see nothing of them.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us automatically. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests of any kind.

Two things are worth stating plainly rather than leaving under "nothing":

  • Crash reports. If you have Apple's Share iPhone Analytics switched on in iOS Settings, Apple may send crash reports for apps you use — including this one — to the developer, in Apple's own aggregated form. That is an Apple service you control in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, and it happens whether or not an app asks. RallyOnly contains no crash reporting code of its own.
  • The feedback email. If you tap Send feedback, the app opens a pre-filled mail with the app version, your iOS version, your iPhone model and three counters — how many sessions you have analysed, how many you shared, and how many share sheets you closed without sending. Nothing else: no file names, no dates, no video. It is an ordinary email you can read, edit or delete before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Photo library access

RallyOnly asks for permission to read your photo library so you can pick a video to edit, and to save a finished clip back if you choose to. It reads only the video you select.

Data stored on your device

Finished sessions, clips and their settings are kept in the app's storage so you can reopen them, along with your language choice and the three counters described above. Deleting a session in the app deletes its files; deleting the app removes everything.

None of it is backed up by us. If you delete the app, or lose the phone, the sessions go with it.

Children

RallyOnly is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone automatically.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
