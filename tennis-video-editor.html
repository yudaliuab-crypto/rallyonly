    How it works

    Three steps, no timeline

    A normal video editor makes you scrub through the whole match and cut every point by hand, which takes about as long as playing it. RallyOnly does the cutting.

    1

    Film with the iPhone camera

    Phone behind the baseline, whole court in frame. Where to put it.

    2

    Pick the video in RallyOnly

    It listens for the ball and watches the court to find each rally: about four minutes for a half-hour session on a recent iPhone.

    3

    Watch, fix, share

    The whole match without the waiting, a two-minute reel of the best rallies, and every rally ranked. Move any start or end a few seconds if you want.

    Questions

    FAQ

    What is the easiest way to edit a tennis match video?

    Let software find the rallies. Editing by hand takes about as long as the match; RallyOnly finds every rally on your iPhone and removes the time between points.

    Do I have to upload my video?

    No. RallyOnly analyses the recording on your iPhone and reads it straight from Photos. Nothing is uploaded and it doesn't make a copy of your video.

    Is the demo real?

    Yes. It is a practice session filmed on a phone, cut by the same analysis the app runs, with nothing adjusted by hand. The app isn't perfect: on some sessions it keeps a few seconds of walking or a ball being fed in, which you can trim in the app.

    Is there a free version?

    Four new matches a month are free with every feature; free exports carry a small watermark. RallyOnly Pro removes the limit and the watermark.

    Does it work for doubles, or for video from a GoPro?

    Yes to both, as long as the camera sees the whole court and the video is in your Photos library.

    Android, Windows or in the browser?

    No. RallyOnly is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later). The demo above plays in any browser, but cutting your own match needs the app.
